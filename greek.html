🇬🇷 The Modern Greek Reading List 🇬🇷



Beginners

Ξενοδοχείο Ατλαντίς, παρακαλώ

Set in a seaside hotel during the busy Greek summer, this lighthearted mystery follows the staff and guests as strange incidents start disrupting the holiday season.

Το περίπτερο στην Αριστοτέλους

Stavroula and Manolis run a busy street kiosk in the heart of Thessaloniki, watching daily life pass by, until an unexpected event turns their quiet routine upside down.

Λυδία: Ένα καλοκαίρι στην Ελλάδα

Lydia is a young woman spending her summer in Greece, navigating cafes, beaches, local traditions, and making new friends.

Continuers


Κανάλι 35

When strange broadcasts and clues begin appearing on a local television channel, a group of young people gets pulled into a mystery.

Η μωβ ομπρέλα

Set in Athens on the eve of World War II, Eleftheria and her twin brothers navigate family life, school, and growing political tension under the shadow of a mysterious neighbor with a purple umbrella.

Για την άλλη πατρίδα

This story explores the lives of migrant and refugee teenagers trying to build a new life and sense of belonging in Greece.


Ένα παιδί μετράει τ' άστρα

Melios, a poor orphaned boy with a thirst for knowledge, leaves his rural village for the city in pursuit of an education.

Γιατί σκότωσα την καλύτερή μου φίλη

After moving from Nigeria back to Athens as a teenager in the 1970s, Maria forms an intense, complicated friendship with her classmate Anna as they navigate adolescence and changing times.

το ψέμα

Insecure and struggling to cope with her new, reduced circumstances, Christina creates a web of lies to present a false image to her new classmates.
