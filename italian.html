🇮🇹 The Italian Reading List 🇮🇹



Beginners

Favole al Telefono

A traveling accountant calls his daughter every night to tell fantastical, short bedtime stories over the phone, each lasting only as long as a single coin allows.

Short Stories in Italian for Beginners

The book contains eight short stories, designed for Italian learners. Readers get the opputunity to learn new langauge as they progress through the book.

Le Avventure di Pinocchio

A mischievous marionette must navigate dangerous trials and his own selfishness in an attempt to become a real boy.

Continuers


Io non ho paura

Set in the scorching heat of a Southern Italian summer in 1978, nine-year-old Michele discovers a dark secret hidden in a hole in the ground near an abandoned farmhouse.

Bianca come il latte, rossa come il sangu

Leo is a typical 16-year-old who hates school, loves soccer, and is secretly in love with Beatrice. When he discovers Beatrice has leukemia, Leo has to grow up fast and confront life's biggest questions.

Novecento

The story of Danny Boodmann T.D. Lemon Novecento, an extraordinary pianist born on an ocean liner who lives his entire life at sea, never once setting foot on land.
